GRANTED
Tells your organisation which funding to go for, and which to leave alone.
==========================================================================

Most funding tools help you write more applications. Granted helps you write
fewer. Every morning it checks open funding calls against what each funder has
actually funded before, and against your own track record. Most days it says
nothing. When a call genuinely fits, it opens a folder for it with a decision,
a draft answer and a timeline, ready for you to edit.


1. INSTALL IT (once on each computer that will use this folder)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Windows:    double-click  Install Granted (Windows).bat
            If Windows says it protected your PC, click More info, then
            Run anyway.
Mac/Linux:  open Terminal in this folder and type  ./install.sh

The installer asks four things:
  - How Granted should reach its AI model. An Anthropic API key is the
    simplest (console.anthropic.com); an AWS account with Amazon Bedrock
    also works. The key is saved on this computer only, never in this
    folder.
  - Your organisation's name.
  - Where your past funding applications are. Word, PDF and text files all
    work. Press Enter to use the "Past applications" folder in here.
  - Whether to run every morning at 8.

It needs Python 3.11 or newer. On Windows it offers to install it for you.


2. CHECK ORG.md
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Granted drafts ORG.md from your past applications: who you are, what you do,
what you have achieved. It lists what it could not find. Open it (on Windows:
right-click, Open with, Notepad), fill the gaps, and correct anything wrong.
Put your postcode on the Postcode line: it decides which regional and local
funding applies to you.

ORG.md is the only file you maintain. Every fact Granted puts in a draft
comes from a line in it, so a trustee can check any figure.


3. RUN IT, THEN OPEN THE DASHBOARD
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Windows:    double-click  Run Granted now.bat
Mac/Linux:  ./run-granted.sh

Then double-click Dashboard.html. It opens in your browser and shows what
Granted looked at and what it decided. Leave it open: it picks up each new
run within a minute. It needs an internet connection.


WHAT IS IN THIS FOLDER
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Dashboard.html       The dashboard. Updates itself after every run.
Bid folders.html     Every bid folder and its files. Works offline.
Bids                 One folder per call worth your time, named by date,
                     funder and programme. Inside each:
                       01-decision    Decision.docx: fit, suggested ask, why
                       02-direction   Direction.docx: the angle to lead with
                       03-drafts      Draft answer v1.docx: edit this one
                       04-evidence    Evidence and sources.docx
                       05-timeline    Timeline.docx: who does what, by when
                       06-submitted   outcome.meta.json: record the result
Daily notes          What each run looked at, and why it stayed quiet.
Past applications    Your past bids, if you keep them here.
ORG.md               About your organisation. Yours to maintain.
granted.json         Settings. Change them in Notepad if you need to.
funders.json         Funders whose award histories Granted can check.
.granted             The program and Granted's memory. Leave it alone.


WHERE THE CALLS COME FROM
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Government grants (Find a grant, GOV.UK), the National Lottery Community
Fund's open programmes, and Innovate UK's competitions. Calls for another
part of the UK, or for businesses only, are ruled out straight away.

Local funders, such as your community foundation and council, do not list
their calls in one place. Once a week Granted checks which of them have
actually funded organisations near you, and lists them on the dashboard and
in the daily note, with links. Their calls are on their own websites.


MOST DAYS IT SAYS NOTHING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
That is the point. A quiet day means nothing open was worth thirty hours of
your time. The daily note says what was checked and why each call stopped,
so you can see it looked.


WHEN YOU HEAR BACK FROM A FUNDER
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Open the bid's 06-submitted\outcome.meta.json in Notepad and change
"pending" to "awarded" or "rejected". That is how Granted learns your track
record, so next year it knows which funders say yes to you.


WHAT LEAVES YOUR COMPUTER
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Granted reads only the past-applications folder you give it. It skips files
whose names suggest safeguarding, referral or personal records, and it never
changes your files.

When a call is worth drafting, it sends the AI model the call, ORG.md, and a
summary of your writing style, which can include short passages from your
past applications. It also looks up public data on funders (360Giving), open
calls (Find a grant, the National Lottery Community Fund, Innovate UK) and
the region your postcode is in (postcodes.io). Granted sends nothing else
anywhere.


MOVING OR SHARING THIS FOLDER
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
The folder can live in OneDrive, SharePoint or Dropbox, and it can be moved.
Each computer that uses it needs the installer once. Only one computer should
run it on a schedule, or two will write to the same folder at once.
