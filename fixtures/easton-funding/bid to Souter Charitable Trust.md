Our members are not statistics to us. We know their names, and we know when they stop coming.

Demand rose. It rose again. We have not turned anyone away yet and we would like to keep it that way. An independent evaluation by Bristol Impact Partnership, published in July 2025, looked at the meals programme across two years and found retention well above what comparable projects report.

We work in Easton, Lawrence Hill and St Pauls. We do not work outside Bristol, and we say so when funders ask us to.

We are applying to Souter Charitable Trust because our work sits squarely within what you fund, and because we would rather ask once, properly, than send the same letter everywhere.

This money would go on food, heat and the van. Nothing else.
