We have run community meals from St Mark's Hall since 2019.

Demand rose. It rose again. We have not turned anyone away yet and we would like to keep it that way. Our Trustee board reviewed this application in full before submission.

Of the 210 members we surveyed, 78 per cent told us they now eat at least one hot meal a day more often than they did before they found us. We work in Easton, Lawrence Hill and St Pauls. We do not work outside Bristol, and we say so when funders ask us to. The Pantry costs members £4.50 a week and returns about £22 of food, which for a household on Universal Credit is the difference between a full week and a thin one.

We are applying to Postcode Local Trust because our work sits squarely within what you fund, and because we would rather ask once, properly, than send the same letter everywhere.

This money would go on food, heat and the van. Nothing else.
