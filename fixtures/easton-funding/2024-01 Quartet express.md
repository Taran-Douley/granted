Easton Community Kitchen serves three sittings a week, every week, without referral.

We work in Easton, Lawrence Hill and St Pauls. We do not work outside Bristol, and we say so when funders ask us to. An independent evaluation by Bristol Impact Partnership, published in July 2025, looked at the meals programme across two years and found retention well above what comparable projects report.

Of the 210 members we surveyed, 78 per cent told us they now eat at least one hot meal a day more often than they did before they found us.

We are applying to Quartet Community Foundation because our work sits squarely within what you fund, and because we would rather ask once, properly, than send the same letter everywhere.

We would be glad to talk this through with you.
