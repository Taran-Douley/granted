We are asking for support to keep the Pantry open through next winter.

The Pantry costs members £4.50 a week and returns about £22 of food, which for a household on Universal Credit is the difference between a full week and a thin one. We are small. Three paid staff, 2.2 full time equivalent, and a Trustee board that meets every six weeks.

Our Trustee board reviewed this application in full before submission. Last year we served 8,900 meals across 156 sittings. Forty-one volunteers did most of it. Of the 210 members we surveyed, 78 per cent told us they now eat at least one hot meal a day more often than they did before they found us.

This money would go on food, heat and the van. Nothing else.
