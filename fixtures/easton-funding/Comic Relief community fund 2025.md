We are asking for support to keep the Pantry open through next winter.

Last year we served 8,900 meals across 156 sittings. Forty-one volunteers did most of it. Of the 210 members we surveyed, 78 per cent told us they now eat at least one hot meal a day more often than they did before they found us.

We are small. Three paid staff, 2.2 full time equivalent, and a Trustee board that meets every six weeks.

We are applying to Comic Relief because our work sits squarely within what you fund, and because we would rather ask once, properly, than send the same letter everywhere.

This money would go on food, heat and the van. Nothing else.
