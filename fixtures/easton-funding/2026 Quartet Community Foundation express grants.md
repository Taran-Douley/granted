We are asking for support to keep the Pantry open through next winter.

Demand rose. It rose again. We have not turned anyone away yet and we would like to keep it that way. The Pantry costs members £4.50 a week and returns about £22 of food, which for a household on Universal Credit is the difference between a full week and a thin one.

Last year we served 8,900 meals across 156 sittings. Forty-one volunteers did most of it.

We are applying to Quartet Community Foundation because our work sits squarely within what you fund, and because we would rather ask once, properly, than send the same letter everywhere.

We would be glad to talk this through with you.
