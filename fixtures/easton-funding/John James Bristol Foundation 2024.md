We have run community meals from St Mark's Hall since 2019.

The Pantry costs members £4.50 a week and returns about £22 of food, which for a household on Universal Credit is the difference between a full week and a thin one. We are small. Three paid staff, 2.2 full time equivalent, and a Trustee board that meets every six weeks.

We work in Easton, Lawrence Hill and St Pauls. We do not work outside Bristol, and we say so when funders ask us to. Our Trustee board reviewed this application in full before submission. Last year we served 8,900 meals across 156 sittings. Forty-one volunteers did most of it.

We are applying to John James Bristol Foundation because our work sits squarely within what you fund, and because we would rather ask once, properly, than send the same letter everywhere.

We have costed this carefully and we can evidence every figure in it.
