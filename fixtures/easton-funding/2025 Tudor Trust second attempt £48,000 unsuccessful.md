Our members are not statistics to us. We know their names, and we know when they stop coming.

An independent evaluation by Bristol Impact Partnership, published in July 2025, looked at the meals programme across two years and found retention well above what comparable projects report. Last year we served 8,900 meals across 156 sittings. Forty-one volunteers did most of it.

We are small. Three paid staff, 2.2 full time equivalent, and a Trustee board that meets every six weeks.

We are applying to Tudor Trust because our work sits squarely within what you fund, and because we would rather ask once, properly, than send the same letter everywhere.

This money would go on food, heat and the van. Nothing else.
