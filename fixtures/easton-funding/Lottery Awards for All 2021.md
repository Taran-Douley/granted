We are asking for support to keep the Pantry open through next winter.

Last year we served 8,900 meals across 156 sittings. Forty-one volunteers did most of it. Of the 210 members we surveyed, 78 per cent told us they now eat at least one hot meal a day more often than they did before they found us.

Demand rose. It rose again. We have not turned anyone away yet and we would like to keep it that way.

We are applying to National Lottery Community Fund because our work sits squarely within what you fund, and because we would rather ask once, properly, than send the same letter everywhere.

We have costed this carefully and we can evidence every figure in it.
