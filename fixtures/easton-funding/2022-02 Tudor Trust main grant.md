Easton Community Kitchen serves three sittings a week, every week, without referral.

The Pantry costs members £4.50 a week and returns about £22 of food, which for a household on Universal Credit is the difference between a full week and a thin one. Last year we served 8,900 meals across 156 sittings. Forty-one volunteers did most of it.

An independent evaluation by Bristol Impact Partnership, published in July 2025, looked at the meals programme across two years and found retention well above what comparable projects report. We work in Easton, Lawrence Hill and St Pauls. We do not work outside Bristol, and we say so when funders ask us to. Demand rose. It rose again. We have not turned anyone away yet and we would like to keep it that way.

We are applying to Tudor Trust because our work sits squarely within what you fund, and because we would rather ask once, properly, than send the same letter everywhere.

This money would go on food, heat and the van. Nothing else.
