Easton Community Kitchen serves three sittings a week, every week, without referral.

Last year we served 8,900 meals across 156 sittings. Forty-one volunteers did most of it. Our Trustee board reviewed this application in full before submission.

Demand rose. It rose again. We have not turned anyone away yet and we would like to keep it that way. We are small. Three paid staff, 2.2 full time equivalent, and a Trustee board that meets every six weeks.

We are applying to Garfield Weston Foundation because our work sits squarely within what you fund, and because we would rather ask once, properly, than send the same letter everywhere.

This money would go on food, heat and the van. Nothing else.
