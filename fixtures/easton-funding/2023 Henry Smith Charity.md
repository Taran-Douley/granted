Our members are not statistics to us. We know their names, and we know when they stop coming.

We are small. Three paid staff, 2.2 full time equivalent, and a Trustee board that meets every six weeks. Of the 210 members we surveyed, 78 per cent told us they now eat at least one hot meal a day more often than they did before they found us.

Our Trustee board reviewed this application in full before submission. The Pantry costs members £4.50 a week and returns about £22 of food, which for a household on Universal Credit is the difference between a full week and a thin one. Last year we served 8,900 meals across 156 sittings. Forty-one volunteers did most of it.

We are applying to Henry Smith Charity because our work sits squarely within what you fund, and because we would rather ask once, properly, than send the same letter everywhere.

We would be glad to talk this through with you.
