We are asking for support to keep the Pantry open through next winter.

Our Trustee board reviewed this application in full before submission. An independent evaluation by Bristol Impact Partnership, published in July 2025, looked at the meals programme across two years and found retention well above what comparable projects report.

Of the 210 members we surveyed, 78 per cent told us they now eat at least one hot meal a day more often than they did before they found us. We are small. Three paid staff, 2.2 full time equivalent, and a Trustee board that meets every six weeks.

We are applying to Bristol City Council because our work sits squarely within what you fund, and because we would rather ask once, properly, than send the same letter everywhere.

This money would go on food, heat and the van. Nothing else.
