We are asking for help to hold the Pantry open through the winter.

We served 7,400 meals over 148 sittings last year. Thirty-eight volunteers carried most of that. Of the 180 members who answered our survey, 74 per cent said they now eat a hot meal daily more often than they managed before they came to us.

We are a small operation. Three paid staff, two full time equivalent, and a Trustee board that sits every six weeks without fail.

We are applying to Comic Relief because our work sits squarely within what you fund, and because we would rather ask once, properly, than send the same letter everywhere.

This grant would pay for food, fuel and the fridge. That is all.
