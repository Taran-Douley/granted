We are asking for help to hold the Pantry open through the winter.

Our Trustee board read this application end to end before it was sent. Southwark Impact Partnership evaluated the meals programme independently and published in June 2025, finding retention stronger than comparable projects across the borough report.

Of the 180 members who answered our survey, 74 per cent said they now eat a hot meal daily more often than they managed before they came to us. We are a small operation. Three paid staff, two full time equivalent, and a Trustee board that sits every six weeks without fail.

We are applying to The London Community Foundation because our work sits squarely within what you fund, and because we would rather ask once, properly, than send the same letter everywhere.

This grant would pay for food, fuel and the fridge. That is all.
