We are asking for help to hold the Pantry open through the winter.

The Pantry costs members £4.50 a week and sends them home with roughly £22 of food, which on a fixed income is the difference between managing and not. We are a small operation. Three paid staff, two full time equivalent, and a Trustee board that sits every six weeks without fail.

Our Trustee board read this application end to end before it was sent. We served 7,400 meals over 148 sittings last year. Thirty-eight volunteers carried most of that. Of the 180 members who answered our survey, 74 per cent said they now eat a hot meal daily more often than they managed before they came to us.

This grant would pay for food, fuel and the fridge. That is all.
