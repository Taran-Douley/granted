We are asking for help to hold the Pantry open through the winter.

We served 7,400 meals over 148 sittings last year. Thirty-eight volunteers carried most of that. Of the 180 members who answered our survey, 74 per cent said they now eat a hot meal daily more often than they managed before they came to us.

Referrals climbed. Then they climbed again. We have not closed the list yet.

We are applying to National Lottery Community Fund because our work sits squarely within what you fund, and because we would rather ask once, properly, than send the same letter everywhere.

Every figure here is costed and we can show you the working.
