Rye Lane Community Kitchen runs three sittings a week and turns nobody away at the door.

The Pantry costs members £4.50 a week and sends them home with roughly £22 of food, which on a fixed income is the difference between managing and not. Of the 180 members who answered our survey, 74 per cent said they now eat a hot meal daily more often than they managed before they came to us.

Referrals climbed. Then they climbed again. We have not closed the list yet. We are a small operation. Three paid staff, two full time equivalent, and a Trustee board that sits every six weeks without fail.

We are applying to Trussell Trust because our work sits squarely within what you fund, and because we would rather ask once, properly, than send the same letter everywhere.

Every figure here is costed and we can show you the working.
