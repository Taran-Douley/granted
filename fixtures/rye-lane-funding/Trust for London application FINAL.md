Rye Lane Community Kitchen runs three sittings a week and turns nobody away at the door.

We served 7,400 meals over 148 sittings last year. Thirty-eight volunteers carried most of that. Our Trustee board read this application end to end before it was sent.

Referrals climbed. Then they climbed again. We have not closed the list yet. We are a small operation. Three paid staff, two full time equivalent, and a Trustee board that sits every six weeks without fail.

We are applying to Trust for London because our work sits squarely within what you fund, and because we would rather ask once, properly, than send the same letter everywhere.

This grant would pay for food, fuel and the fridge. That is all.
