We are asking for help to hold the Pantry open through the winter.

Referrals climbed. Then they climbed again. We have not closed the list yet. The Pantry costs members £4.50 a week and sends them home with roughly £22 of food, which on a fixed income is the difference between managing and not.

We served 7,400 meals over 148 sittings last year. Thirty-eight volunteers carried most of that.

We are applying to The London Community Foundation because our work sits squarely within what you fund, and because we would rather ask once, properly, than send the same letter everywhere.

We would welcome the chance to walk you through this in person.
