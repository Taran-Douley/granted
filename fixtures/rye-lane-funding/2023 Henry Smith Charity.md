Our members know us by name, and we notice the week somebody stops coming.

We are a small operation. Three paid staff, two full time equivalent, and a Trustee board that sits every six weeks without fail. Of the 180 members who answered our survey, 74 per cent said they now eat a hot meal daily more often than they managed before they came to us.

Our Trustee board read this application end to end before it was sent. The Pantry costs members £4.50 a week and sends them home with roughly £22 of food, which on a fixed income is the difference between managing and not. We served 7,400 meals over 148 sittings last year. Thirty-eight volunteers carried most of that.

We are applying to Henry Smith Charity because our work sits squarely within what you fund, and because we would rather ask once, properly, than send the same letter everywhere.

We would welcome the chance to walk you through this in person.
