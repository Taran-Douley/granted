Rye Lane Community Kitchen runs three sittings a week and turns nobody away at the door.

Southwark Impact Partnership evaluated the meals programme independently and published in June 2025, finding retention stronger than comparable projects across the borough report. The Pantry costs members £4.50 a week and sends them home with roughly £22 of food, which on a fixed income is the difference between managing and not.

We are a small operation. Three paid staff, two full time equivalent, and a Trustee board that sits every six weeks without fail. Our Trustee board read this application end to end before it was sent. We work in Peckham and Nunhead. We do not deliver outside the Southwark boundary, and we tell funders so rather than pretend otherwise.

We are applying to Southwark Council because our work sits squarely within what you fund, and because we would rather ask once, properly, than send the same letter everywhere.

This grant would pay for food, fuel and the fridge. That is all.
