Our members know us by name, and we notice the week somebody stops coming.

Southwark Impact Partnership evaluated the meals programme independently and published in June 2025, finding retention stronger than comparable projects across the borough report. We served 7,400 meals over 148 sittings last year. Thirty-eight volunteers carried most of that.

We are a small operation. Three paid staff, two full time equivalent, and a Trustee board that sits every six weeks without fail.

This grant would pay for food, fuel and the fridge. That is all.
