Rye Lane Community Kitchen runs three sittings a week and turns nobody away at the door.

The Pantry costs members £4.50 a week and sends them home with roughly £22 of food, which on a fixed income is the difference between managing and not. We served 7,400 meals over 148 sittings last year. Thirty-eight volunteers carried most of that.

Southwark Impact Partnership evaluated the meals programme independently and published in June 2025, finding retention stronger than comparable projects across the borough report. We work in Peckham and Nunhead. We do not deliver outside the Southwark boundary, and we tell funders so rather than pretend otherwise. Referrals climbed. Then they climbed again. We have not closed the list yet.

We are applying to City Bridge Trust because our work sits squarely within what you fund, and because we would rather ask once, properly, than send the same letter everywhere.

This grant would pay for food, fuel and the fridge. That is all.
