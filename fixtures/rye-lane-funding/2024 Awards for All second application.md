Rye Lane Community Kitchen runs three sittings a week and turns nobody away at the door.

Referrals climbed. Then they climbed again. We have not closed the list yet. We served 7,400 meals over 148 sittings last year. Thirty-eight volunteers carried most of that.

The Pantry costs members £4.50 a week and sends them home with roughly £22 of food, which on a fixed income is the difference between managing and not. We are a small operation. Three paid staff, two full time equivalent, and a Trustee board that sits every six weeks without fail. Our Trustee board read this application end to end before it was sent.

We are applying to National Lottery Community Fund because our work sits squarely within what you fund, and because we would rather ask once, properly, than send the same letter everywhere.

Every figure here is costed and we can show you the working.
