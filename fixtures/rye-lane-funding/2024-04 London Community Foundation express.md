Rye Lane Community Kitchen runs three sittings a week and turns nobody away at the door.

We work in Peckham and Nunhead. We do not deliver outside the Southwark boundary, and we tell funders so rather than pretend otherwise. Southwark Impact Partnership evaluated the meals programme independently and published in June 2025, finding retention stronger than comparable projects across the borough report.

Of the 180 members who answered our survey, 74 per cent said they now eat a hot meal daily more often than they managed before they came to us.

We are applying to The London Community Foundation because our work sits squarely within what you fund, and because we would rather ask once, properly, than send the same letter everywhere.

We would welcome the chance to walk you through this in person.
