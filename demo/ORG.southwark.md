- Volume 2025-26: 7,400 meals served across 148 sittings
- Outcome: 74% of surveyed members (n=180) reported eating at least one hot

- Delivery: membership model, £4.50 a week for approximately £22 of food
- Volume 2025-26: 190 member households, 58% retention year on year
- Outcome: members reported an average £61 monthly reduction in food spend
  (self-reported, n=74, survey ran Jan 2026)
